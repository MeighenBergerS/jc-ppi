# Iowa Particles & Plots Journal Club

A weekly discussion of high-energy physics papers at the University of Iowa. Members suggest
papers during the week and vote for the ones they want to discuss; the meeting time is on the
website.

- **Website:** <https://meighenbergers.github.io/jc-ppi/>, with this week's papers, the
  archive, trending papers and stats.
- **Papers:** each suggestion is a GitHub issue labelled `paper`,
  <https://github.com/MeighenBergerS/jc-ppi/issues?q=label%3Apaper>.

- [arXiv and INSPIRE-HEP](ARXIV-GUIDE.md): finding papers and reading what the site shows about
