Anyone can bring a paper

You don’t need to be an expert, to have read everything in the field, or to think a paper is important. If you’re curious about it, bring it. New here? Your suggestions are especially welcome.

Any of these is a good reason:

  • 🔍 “I don’t understand this result.”
  • 💡 “This is a clever idea.”
  • 🤔 “I think this argument might be wrong.”
  • 🆕 “This just came out and I’d like to hear what people think.”
  • 🔗 “This connects two things we don’t usually discuss together.”
